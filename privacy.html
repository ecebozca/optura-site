Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how we handle the information you share with us when you join the Optura early access list on this website. Optura is an early-stage product operated by Ece Bozca. If you have any questions, email ece.bozca@gmail.com.

What we collect

On this website we only collect the email address you choose to submit when joining the early access list. We do not ask for, or collect, your kitchen inventory or any other household data on this website.

Why we collect it

We use your email address to:

  • Send occasional updates about Optura's progress.
  • Invite you to try Optura when early testing opportunities become available.

We send these messages only about Optura. We don't sell or rent your email address to anyone.

Legal basis

We process your email address on the basis of your consent, which you give by submitting the form. You can withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time (see “Your choices” below).

Who processes it

Our signup form is powered by Formspree, a third-party service that receives and stores form submissions on our behalf. Your email address is stored on Formspree's servers, which may be located outside the European Economic Area (for example, in the United States).

Web fonts on this site are served by Google Fonts. The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies needed for it to function, plus your saved cookie choice. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If we introduce analytics in the future, we'll ask for your consent first.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you ask us to remove it, or until the early access programme ends and the list is no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

You can, at any time:

  • Ask us what data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your email address.
  • Withdraw your consent and unsubscribe from the list.

To do any of these, just email ece.bozca@gmail.com. If you're in the EU/EEA and believe your data isn't handled properly, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

As Optura develops, we may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

This is an early-stage website for a product that is still in development. It describes how we handle the email you share to join the waitlist — not a finished app. Feature and pricing plans mentioned elsewhere on the site are not final.
