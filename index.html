Food waste often begins before
anything is thrown away.

It begins at an ordinary decision moment: What should I make with what I already have?

This is where Optura helps.

How it works

 

Intervene
before
food becomes
waste.

One continuous loop from what you buy to what you save.

    01 / Capture

    Add what you buy.

    Snap your groceries or scan your receipt. Optura builds and organises your inventory automatically.

    • Scan your receipt

      Turn your grocery receipt into a digital inventory in seconds.

    • Automatic item recognition

      Optura identifies products and quantities for you.

    • Smart expiry estimates

      Each item gets an estimated consumption window automatically.

    02 / Track

    Know what needs
    attention

    Optura keeps track of what you have, what to use first, and when, so nothing gets forgotten.

    • Always-updated inventory

      See what you have at home and how much is left.

    • Smart prioritisation

      Items are ranked by urgency, so you know what to use first.

    • At-risk alerts

      Get notified before food is likely to go to waste.

    03 / Act

    Turn risk into a
    meal

    Optura turns what needs attention into timely, personalised actions that help you decide what to cook before food goes to waste.

    • Personalised meal suggestions

      Get recipe ideas based on what needs to be used first.

    • Cook with what you have

      Recipes adapt to the ingredients already in your kitchen.

    • Timely interventions

      Get the right suggestion when an ingredient needs your attention.

    04 / Reward

    See every save
    add up

    Turn food-saving actions into measurable impact, points, and real-world rewards.

    • Earn points as you save

      Get rewarded for using food before it goes to waste.

    • Build your streak

      Turn small, consistent actions into a lasting habit.

    • Unlock real-world rewards

      Redeem your points for offers from grocery and delivery partners.

    • See your impact

      Track the food, money, and CO₂ you save over time.
